CRC

Cyborg Resilience Co-lab

Research × art × technology: a platform for working across fields

Mei-chun Lee, an associate research fellow at the Institute of Ethnology, Academia Sinica, founded CRC in July 2025. CRC is a working network that links research with practice, rather than a traditional research center. War, disaster, and technology risks now overlap, and CRC brings people together to understand digital resilience and put it into practice.
