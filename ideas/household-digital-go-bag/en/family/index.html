All of it takes one weekend afternoon

You don't need tech skills for this page. The times are on the left. Do the tasks in any order, and check each one off when it's done.All of it costs far less than installing an air conditioner.

How longWhat to doDoneWho
5 minutesDownload offline (Without the internet. You can still open it and use it.) maps on everyone's phone (three areas: home, work, and your family's hometown)
5 minutesPick 10 key phone numbers, copy them onto paper, and put it on the fridge
10 minutesPrint the 2FA backup codes (Spare codes for two-step sign-in. When you sign in, you type your password and then a code sent to your phone. A backup code is a one-time code you can use instead when your phone isn't with you.) for all your accounts and keep them with your bank passbook
15 minutesAgree on a meetup rule for outages: if the internet is down for more than half a day, where and when you'll meet
20 minutesIn Google Drive Desktop (The Google Drive program you install on a computer. It keeps folders on the computer in sync with the cloud.), switch your family's important folders to Mirror (Both sides always match. Change something on one side, and the other side changes too.) (the whole folder syncs both ways, automatically)
30 minutesBuy a portable hard drive (A small storage device you plug into a computer. It doesn't need the internet.), drag your "can't lose" folders onto it, and write the date on it. Next time you visit your family's hometown, leave it with a relative
Compare pricesGet a ~300Wh portable power station (A small box with a battery inside. You can plug things into it. Wh (watt-hours) measures how much power it holds. The bigger the number, the longer it lasts.) for your home (or at least a large power bank) to keep the router and phones running through a blackout night

Things to read, watch, and hear offline: save them now

It's 8 p.m., and the internet is down. No streaming. No podcasts (Talk shows on the internet. Think of radio programs you can play anytime.). The kids are restless, and the grown-ups are anxious.
Getting through those evenings is part of resilience too.

20albums offlineDownload your 20 favorite albums first, or buy the files and keep them on a drive
50videos offlineKeep some documentaries, how-to videos, and shows for the kids
12e-booksSave epub (A file type for e-books. Phones and computers can both open it.) files on your device. The real insurance is paper books: you can read them in a blackout

One weekend of downloading does it, and you don't have to wait for an outage to enjoy them.
