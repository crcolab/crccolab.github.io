**English translation in progress.** We are translating this guide and will publish it here when it is ready.

The Household Digital Go-Bag rebuilds the decks and worksheets from CRC's workshop on internet outages and where they come from as a why-and-how guide. It covers what happens to a household's digital life when the internet, Taiwan's international links, your ISP or the power goes down, and how to prepare for it.

In the meantime, the [Traditional Chinese version](/ideas/household-digital-go-bag/) is available now.
