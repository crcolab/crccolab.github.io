Doublethink Lab ✕ Watchout ✕ CRC Cyborg Resilience Co-lab

🚨 What is "grey-zone" warfare?

Many people assume a war begins with missiles landing and air-raid sirens blaring. But in modern hybrid warfare, an adversary's most common approach is grey-zone operations — more covert and harder to guard against — including subsea cable disruption, cyber intrusion and data theft, and cognitive manipulation.

They leave us feeling worn out and powerless about the present without even noticing, sinking into learned helplessness.

Their true aim is to "gradually change the strategic status quo without triggering full-scale military conflict, dismantling Taiwan's internal will to resist step by step."

▍What you will learn at this forum:

- Seeing through threats: dissecting the newest grey-zone cyber operation tactics of 2026.
- Mental defense: building a personal line of cognitive defense against information warfare and emotional manipulation, and rejecting black-and-white extreme narratives.
- Blackout backup plans: hands-on analysis of a 30-minute connection-throttling drill! A rundown of offline tools and practical fallbacks for when critical communications are blocked.
- Interactive experience: try young creators' knowledge-translation works in person, letting preparedness awareness blend naturally into daily life.
