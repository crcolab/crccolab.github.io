[CRC](/en/)’s [Meichun Lee](/en/team/meichun/) and [CHENG PENG](/en/team/cheng/) have been invited to an event co-hosted by the [Open Culture Foundation (OCF)](https://ocf.tw/){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [ARTICLE 19](https://www.article19.org/){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [DSET](https://dset.tw/){: target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. Meichun Lee joins speakers from the Philippines and South Korea for the Session 2 panel, and CHENG PENG speaks in Session 1 on “Is Hosting in Taiwan Enough? Can Digital Services Stay Up When the Internet Disrupt?” The event details and programme below are as published by the organisers.

An event on how civil society in Taiwan and the Indo-Pacific can help protect digital critical infrastructure under democratic governance.

## Event details

Date: Friday, 4 December 2026\\
Time: 13:15-17:00 (registration opens at 13:00)\\
Venue: NCCU Public Administration and Business Executive Center (公企中心), Taipei\\
Co-hosts: Open Culture Foundation (OCF), ARTICLE 19 and DSET\\
Languages: Mandarin (Session 1) and English (Session 2), with Mandarin-English interpretation

## Registration

Deadline: Friday, 27 November 2026\\
Seats are limited. The organisers will review each registration and email confirmed participants with the room details.\\
Contact: Liu I-Chen, ARTICLE 19

- 13:00-13:15　Registration

## Ground rules

The workshop is held under the Chatham House Rule: participants may use the information received, but may not reveal the identity or affiliation of any speaker or participant. Session 1 is not open for quotation or media interviews. Journalists who wish to report other remarks must ask the speaker privately for consent. All participants attend in a personal capacity.
